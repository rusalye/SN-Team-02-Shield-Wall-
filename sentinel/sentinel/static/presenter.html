The problem and our answer

A legacy web app (OWASP Juice Shop) cannot be patched this week. We protect it at the edge, with controls that need no change to the app.

Prevent. TLS termination, ModSecurity v3 with OWASP CRS at paranoia level 2, three virtual patches for real Juice Shop flaws, decoy honeypot paths, per-IP rate limiting.
Respond. Sentinel reads the WAF logs, scores each source, bans repeat offenders with escalating durations (5 min, 30 min, 24 h), and reloads nginx with no Docker socket. Prove. Every alert is mapped to ATT&CK, CWE and CAPEC.
